# 🏊‍♀️ Glide Swimming Lessons Website

A beautiful, water-themed website for Glide Swimming Lessons featuring watercolor-inspired design and smooth animations.

## ✨ Features

- **Responsive Design**: Works perfectly on desktop, tablet, and mobile devices
- **Watercolor Aesthetic**: Beautiful blue watercolor-inspired styling with flowing animations
- **Interactive Elements**: Smooth scrolling, hover effects, and form validation
- **Modern UI/UX**: Clean, professional design with excellent user experience
- **Contact Form**: Fully functional contact form with validation
- **Photo Gallery**: Placeholder gallery ready for your swimming lesson photos
- **Course Schedule**: Clear display of lesson times and age groups

## 🎨 Design Highlights

- **Water-themed Colors**: Various shades of blue with gradient backgrounds
- **Floating Animations**: Subtle water ripple effects and floating elements
- **Watercolor Blobs**: Abstract watercolor-style background elements
- **Smooth Transitions**: Elegant animations throughout the site
- **Modern Typography**: Clean, readable fonts with proper hierarchy

## 📱 Sections

1. **Hero Section**: Eye-catching introduction with call-to-action
2. **What is Glide**: Information about the swimming program
3. **Lesson Courses**: Schedule and course information
4. **Photo Gallery**: Visual showcase of swimming lessons
5. **Contact**: Contact form and business information

## 🚀 Getting Started

1. **Clone or Download** the project files
2. **Open** `index.html` in your web browser
3. **Customize** the content to match your specific needs:
   - Update contact information
   - Replace placeholder photos with actual images
   - Modify course schedules and pricing
   - Add your business logo

## 📝 Customization

### Contact Information
Update the contact details in the HTML file:
- Phone number: `(555) 123-GLIDE`
- Email: `info@glideswimming.com`
- Address: `123 Aquatic Drive, Swim City, SC 12345`

### Photos
Replace the placeholder photo gallery with actual images:
- Add your photos to an `images` folder
- Update the `placeholder-photo` divs with `<img>` tags
- Recommended image size: 400x300px

### Course Schedule
Modify the lesson times and days in the courses section to match your actual schedule.

### Colors
The color scheme can be customized by modifying the CSS variables in `styles.css`. The main colors are:
- Primary Blue: `#1976d2`
- Light Blue: `#2196f3`
- Accent Blue: `#21cbf3`
